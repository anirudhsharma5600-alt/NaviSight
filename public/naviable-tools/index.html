About this prototype

About NaviAble AI

What it is

NaviAble AI is an on-demand visual assistance prototype for blind and low-vision users. It starts with printed signs and common objects.

How it works

  1. Open when you need visual information.
  2. Choose Read a sign or Describe objects.
  3. Point the camera for a short scan.
  4. OCR reads the text or the detector names common objects with left/centre/right in the image.
  5. The result is spoken and can be repeated or stopped.

Expected impact

Easier access to these specific tasks through audio. We have not yet proven this impact.

Evaluation

Sign-reading success, object and position results, response time, feedback from blind and low-vision users.

Where it fits

Products such as Meta's Ray-Ban AI glasses and Be My Eyes already offer hands-free visual assistance. NaviAble does not claim to replace them or to have a better AI model. Our phone-first prototype is designed for short, on-demand use without dedicated glasses. Our hypothesis is that this focused workflow is useful, and user testing will tell us whether it is.

Next

Audio guidance for aiming the camera. After validating the phone experience we plan to explore wearable integration where the device platform supports it.

Limits

NaviAble does not provide safe-route guidance or exact object distances.
